# UI review lane 09: responsive geometry

## Audit scope and evidence

Manual review of the synthetic Electron/React renderer fixtures and their direct layout sources. This is screenshot and source evidence, not live-account or full-runtime verification.

Images viewed with `view_image`:

1. `14-settings-small.png` — Settings, English, 860×700 window / 860×668 viewport.
2. `19-settings-ru.png` — Settings, Russian, 860×700 window / 860×668 viewport.
3. `03-accounts.png` — Accounts, English, 1360×960 window / 1360×928 viewport.

The manifest reports `rootOverflow: false` for all three fixtures. The NEKODEX cat mark remains recognizable at both sizes, and the screenshots preserve the distinction between account verification/status, account selection, and actions that can replace credentials.

## Important findings

### 1. [P1] The 820 px window breakpoint leaves a cramped 640 px workspace at 860 px, while the two mode cards refuse to reflow

- **Observed rendering:** in `14-settings-small.png`, the persistent 220 px sidebar leaves the Settings surface with roughly 640 px before gutters. “With Automation” and “Manual mode” remain side by side, making each explanatory card narrow and unusually tall. In `19-settings-ru.png`, the same geometry is materially worse: the longer Russian mode labels nearly touch across the gutter, paragraphs break into many short lines, and the primary capacity control/status is pushed below the fold. The 1360 px Accounts fixture shows that the normal wide-shell proportions are comfortable; this is a middle-width failure, not a general density complaint.
- **Source inference:** `launcher/src/App.tsx:44` and `:728-736` switch to the overlay sidebar only at `max-width: 820px`. `launcher/src/tokens.css:66-68` fixes the sidebar at 220 px and Settings at up to 790 px; `launcher/src/nekodex.css:153` then reserves 64 px of horizontal gutter. `launcher/src/styles.css:1898-1903` always keeps `.interaction-mode-picker` in two equal columns. The existing workspace container rule at `launcher/src/nekodex.css:392-413` adapts other controls at 760 px but does not reflow this picker.
- **Failure trigger:** window widths just above 820 px, especially with Russian or similarly long localization, or increased text size. The shell stays in desktop-sidebar mode even though the actual workspace has crossed into a narrow layout.
- **Precise fix suggestion / owner:** the **shell + Settings responsive owner** should base compact navigation on available workspace width rather than raw window width (or raise the shell threshold to the width needed for 220 px sidebar + a comfortable Settings column). Independently add a workspace container rule that changes `.interaction-mode-picker` to one column once its content area is about 680–720 px or narrower, with `min-height: auto`. Keep the complete Automation/Manual explanations and their radio semantics; do not shorten away the account-policy boundary.

### 2. [P1] At 700 px height, primary Runtime navigation is clipped behind an independently scrolling sidebar region

- **Observed rendering:** `14-settings-small.png` shows the `Runtime` group heading at the bottom of the navigation region but not its `Activity` destination. In `19-settings-ru.png`, the longer brand and navigation labels consume enough height that the entire Runtime group is absent. The fixed session/Updates/Settings/version footer remains visible in both. `03-accounts.png` at 960 px height shows `Runtime → Activity`, confirming that this is vertical reflow/clipping rather than a missing route. No persistent scroll cue is visible in either 700 px capture.
- **Source inference:** `launcher/src/App.tsx:890-947` places Workspace, Configuration, and Runtime in `.sidebar-nav`, followed by a separate non-shrinking footer. `launcher/src/styles.css:364-370` makes the nav the only scrollable flex child, while `:470-474` pins the footer. NEKODEX spacing increases each group margin at `launcher/src/nekodex.css:35-38`; there is no short-height adaptation. The route remains present in source at `launcher/src/App.tsx:925-927`, so this report does not infer a routing failure.
- **Failure trigger:** a 700 px-tall window, longer localization, OS text scaling, or any extra sidebar badge/copy. Users can overlook Activity because a group heading may be left as the last visible item with its only child below the fold.
- **Precise fix suggestion / owner:** the **sidebar responsive owner** should add a short-height mode (for example, `@media (max-height: 760px)`) that reduces brand/group vertical spacing and footer padding enough to keep every primary destination visible. If scrolling is still necessary, retain it but add a visible bottom-edge fade or persistent scrollbar and scroll the active item into view after navigation/localization changes. Preserve the cat/version footer and do not merge account, setup, or runtime destinations in a way that obscures their separate authority boundaries.

## Specific polish recommendations

1. **Subjective preference — reduce the narrow Settings title scale slightly.** In both 860 px captures the 30 px title consumes a large fraction of the first viewport; the Russian title is especially dominant. A workspace-scoped `clamp()` or 26–28 px title below the narrow threshold would expose more of the first actionable setting without weakening hierarchy. This is polish, not evidence of clipping.

2. **Subjective preference with an accessibility benefit — strengthen the Accounts action row.** In `03-accounts.png`, the credential action is clear, but disabled/secondary “Selected”, “Check account”, and “Check connector” controls read like low-contrast inline labels and use the shared 34–36 px control height (`launcher/src/styles.css:1367-1421`, `launcher/src/nekodex.css:168-170`). Keep the credential replacement action visually distinct, but give actionable checks a consistent secondary-button container and about 40 px minimum height; keep verified/selected states as non-action status chips. This preserves the native approval and account-selection boundaries rather than making every item look equally clickable.

## Evidence limits

- **Observed:** static geometry, wrapping, visible controls, and which navigation items appear in the three synthetic captures.
- **Source-inferred:** breakpoint behavior, scroll ownership, and likely behavior between the captured sizes.
- **Unverified live behavior:** mouse/touch hit regions, wheel and keyboard scrolling, focus visibility while the sidebar is scrolled, active-item auto-scroll, actual account checks, credential replacement, and behavior with real account names/data. The synthetic `rootOverflow: false` result confirms no root-level overflow in these fixtures; it does not prove that all nested content is discoverable or that live Electron behavior is correct.
