## Design language

- NEKODEX keeps its cat identity, dark workspace shell, violet selection treatment, and compact sidebar throughout all three captures.
- Localized product copy may translate surrounding instructions, but connector names and external ChatGPT controls that users must match exactly need unmistakable literal treatment.
- The account and approval boundaries are binding content: separate account/workspace, exact connector identity, ChatGPT permission, Codex sandbox/approval, and explicit verification must remain visible after copy edits.
- No exception was found that permits Native6 controls to fall back silently to English in otherwise localized UI.

## Counterevidence and non-findings

- `14-settings-small.png` initially looked horizontally clipped in a reduced preview, but the full 1720×1336 image shows both interaction-mode cards, the complete capacity warning, controls, saved state, and the next section boundary. The manifest also reports `rootOverflow: false`. Density is high at 860×668, but this fixture does not support a clipping or overflow finding.
- `19-settings-ru.png` keeps navigation, Settings headings, interaction modes, capacity warning, save state, and status text in Russian while preserving product names. No visible mixed-language defect is established on that captured Settings viewport.
- `launcher/src/i18n.ts:1523-1534` gives Russian, Korean, and Traditional Chinese full-key dictionaries rather than per-key English fallback. This supports the captured Settings localization and narrows finding 1 to the separate Native6 helper.
- `launcher/src/update-copy.ts:3-80` defines a complete typed update-copy object for every registered language. No assigned update screenshot was provided, so live rendering remains unverified, but there is no source-supported parity finding in this lane.

## Polish recommendations

1. In English Settings, rename `With Automation` to `Automatic mode` so the two visible choices use parallel nouns (`Automatic mode` / `Manual mode`). This is a subjective copy preference supported by `14-settings-small.png` and `launcher/src/i18n.ts:354-357`; it does not change behavior or authorization.
2. Keep the capacity warning directly under “Parallel agents,” but emphasize only the short clause “Does not increase ChatGPT allowance.” The full warning is accurate and should remain available; this is a subjective density improvement, not evidence of a broken layout.

## Evidence boundaries

- **Observed rendering:** the three named synthetic captures, including the dense MCP paragraph and the readable 860px Settings layouts.
- **Source inference:** Native6 fallback behavior and locale-specific mixed external labels. These are deterministic from the selected source but were not rendered in Korean, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese fixtures here.
- **Subjective preference:** the two polish recommendations. They should not be promoted to defects without product-owner selection.
- **Unverified live behavior:** real ChatGPT label localization, actual connector creation, account matching, permission dialogs, connector verification, Native6 tool execution, installed-app rendering, and update rendering. The synthetic fixture and mock state do not prove any of these.

## Improve first

Fix finding 1 first. Native6 is the current connector path, and an English fallback inside four supported localized UIs undermines the exact identity and approval copy precisely where users must distinguish compatibility connectors from an explicit Native6 upgrade.
