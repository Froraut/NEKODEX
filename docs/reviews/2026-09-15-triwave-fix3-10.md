## Correction

`currentJournalBytes()` previously returned `undefined` when a journal copy disappeared between journal parsing and preparation of a guarded compensation write. The shared `writeFilesWithCompensation()` helper interprets an absent `expectedData` as an unguarded write. A concurrent deletion could therefore disable the intended ownership guard and allow a repair or pending-hook recovery to recreate a journal copy that had disappeared.

The helper now fails closed with an explicit journal-changed error when the expected current journal copy is missing. Existing journal repair and pending JSON-hook recovery calls continue to pass exact current bytes as `expectedData`. The change is limited to `src/codex-integration-journal.ts`.

The accepted wave-2 correction remains intact:

- pending JSON-hook recovery guards both the hook bytes and the primary journal bytes;
- v2 marker repair guards both journal copies;
- divergent-copy repair guards both journal copies;
- ordinary inspection callers use `repair: false`;
- explicit disconnect and uninstall retain the active-primary/inactive-recovery requirement before hook reconciliation.
