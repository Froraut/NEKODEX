## Implemented DIDs

- **T1-6-1 / D17 selection ownership:** automatic turn reveal captures the current `selectionRevision`, creates/leases the turn tab without publishing visible selection during the await, and publishes the account/tab only when the revision is still owned by that turn. A later explicit account or tab selection therefore retains ownership. Turn affinity and the helper lease remain pinned to the chosen account.
- **T2-6-1 / D17 selection ownership:** the same post-await ownership gate prevents a newly created or reused automatic turn tab from replacing a later explicit tab choice. The automatic path still creates and leases the tab on its assigned host; it only reveals it when its selection token is current.
- **T1-5-1:** disabling an account immediately removes process-local capability and connector evidence. In-flight account checks and startup refreshes also refuse to repopulate evidence after the account becomes disabled.
- **T2-5-1:** capability reinspection invalidates capability and connector evidence before the attempt and removes both on failure. A successful result is recorded only if the account is still enabled after the awaited inspection. Existing connector evidence is not recreated by capability-only inspection.
- **Logout evidence lifecycle:** the existing logout invalidation remains intact; the updated disable/reinspection paths use the same evidence retirement boundary.
