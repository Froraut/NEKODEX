## Reviewed residual boundaries

- `checkAccount()` invalidates capability and connector evidence before `host.ready()`, keeps publication behind the captured evidence epoch, and publishes the cleared state when readiness, active-operation, inspection, or connector verification fails. Concurrent checks cannot republish an older epoch.
- `beginTurn()` records the selected account and reservation before readiness, then rechecks account existence, enablement, evidence epoch, and fresh-turn eligibility immediately before `host.beginTurn()`. Its `finally` releases the reservation and pending affinity; the catch path preserves ownership when a browser tab was actually created.
- `openAccountLogin()` retains a pool-owned login operation across the async reveal/login call, so account selection is blocked while login starts and the lease is released on both success and failure. The renderer’s split selection/login caller remains protected by the selection revision checks in `selectAccount()` and the pool lease.
- Balanced routing rejects unchecked authenticated secondary accounts, while the selected primary-account compatibility policy and optional connector requirement remain unchanged.
- Direct callers in `main.cjs`, `control-server.cjs`, and `AccountSettings.tsx` use the existing sync/async contracts. No caller-side correction or resource-lifetime change was supported by the current source.
