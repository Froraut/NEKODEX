## Result

Two residual lifecycle errors in the shared wave1 supervisor patch were corrected.
The queue and cancellation ownership now cover the complete tunnel-control
operation, including the interval before a child is spawned. Once a child is
spawned, its controller and queue slot remain owned until `close` settles.

The existing pre-settlement graceful and forced process-tree signals remain in
place, including the `exit`-path descendant cleanup needed before `close`.
`stopChild` still has no unconditional signal after its child has settled.

## New corrections

- `L07-control-operation-cancellation`: forced shutdown previously cancelled
  only controllers registered after child spawn. A queued or proxy-environment
  control operation could retain its slot without being cancellable, allowing
  forced cleanup to proceed while that operation was still active. The
  supervisor now registers the controller before queue reservation, races queue
  waiting and provider resolution against cancellation, waits for all active
  operation controllers, and skips replacement tunnel stop until they settle.
- `L07-control-close-release`: the wave1 outer rejection handler released the
  queue slot and controller even after a child had spawned but before its
  streams emitted `close`. That could overlap a replacement command with a
  still-owned process tree. Release now happens immediately only for pre-spawn
  failures; spawned children release both ownership records from the `close`
  handler.
