## Implemented

Wave 1 already invalidated simple stale evidence cases and added the automatic reveal selection-revision guard. This wave keeps those fixes and closes the remaining cross-scope evidence races from the accepted triwave findings.

### T1-5-1 / T2-5-1 — account evidence epoch

Added a process-local per-account evidence epoch alongside the existing capability and connector maps. Evidence is invalidated and the epoch advances when:

- an account is enabled or disabled;
- an explicit account check starts;
- a startup authentication refresh starts;
- a capability inspection starts;
- logout retires account evidence; or
- a host publishes an unauthenticated state.

Each asynchronous inspection, authentication refresh, and connector verification captures its epoch. It publishes a result only when that epoch is still current and the account is still enabled. A later check, disable/re-enable transition, logout, authentication loss, or competing inspection therefore cannot be overwritten by an older completion.

Failure cleanup is also epoch-aware: an older failed operation cannot delete connector or capability evidence produced by a newer operation. Direct `inspectSession(true, accountId)` now invalidates evidence before the operation and invalidates it again on a current-operation failure, matching the existing `checkAccount()` and refresh semantics.

### Existing wave 1 selection correction retained

The current diff continues to keep automatic turn ownership and visible selection separate while `BrowserHost.beginTurn()` awaits. Reveal publishes the account and tab only when its captured `selectionRevision` remains current, then selects and shows the leased tab synchronously. This preserves a later explicit account/tab selection across the asynchronous turn-start path.

I reviewed the changed epoch helpers, all capability/connector writers and invalidators, the direct control-server inspection path, startup refresh, logout, host-authentication publication, account enable transitions, competing async checks, and the existing turn reveal / selection-revision path.

No additional concrete account-pool interaction defect survived that review. The accepted T1-5-1 and T2-5-1 paths are covered by the epoch guard, including the wave 3 refinement where an in-flight operation completes after disable/re-enable. No optional redesign was introduced.

## Remaining limits

- This is source-level race protection; no live ChatGPT account transition, connector approval/removal, model-entitlement change, retained-task continuation, or completed browser turn was exercised.
- Evidence epochs are process-local and are not persisted. A new process still requires its normal authentication and capability checks.
- The selected primary compatibility path and exact retained/running continuation policy remain unchanged. A retained or running turn may finish on its pinned account even after that account is disabled; fresh pinned turns still use the readiness filter.
- Account-side DOM behavior, browser-host timing, and installed-app/runtime readiness remain outside this lane.
