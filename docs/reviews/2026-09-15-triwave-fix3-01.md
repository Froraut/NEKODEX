- unknown service or tunnel state fails closed and records manual recovery;
- unsafe config rollback prevents dependent service/tunnel restoration;
- service and tunnel compensation remain independent;
- tunnel client and runtime-key cleanup remains conditional on the validation runtime no longer being active;
- a concurrent edit is preserved instead of overwritten.
