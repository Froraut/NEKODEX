## Repeats and known limits

- The old write-before-checkpoint defects are repeats of adjudicated **D01/D02**, already corrected in this HEAD. Re-reporting them would duplicate the prior root.
- A failure after `launchctl bootout` or during the bounded unload/bootstrap sequence can still leave service state requiring manual recovery if launchd cannot be observed or restarted. `src/service.ts:279-290` and `src/tunnel-service.ts:143-167` bound their waits and propagate the failure; setup deliberately refuses to guess ownership when status observation fails (`src/setup.ts:639-659,873-889,896-909`). The prior results explicitly retain this as a safety boundary, not a new source defect.
- `getServiceStatus()` and `getTunnelServiceStatus()` interpret an ordinary nonzero `launchctl print` result as not loaded (`src/service.ts:103-113`, `src/tunnel-service.ts:88-104`). The missing-target nonzero result is required for an unloaded service; source review does not establish a distinct current trigger that would let an unrelated nonzero result be separated safely. Setup catches thrown probe failures/timeouts as unknown. I do not count this as a concrete bug without a demonstrated actionable status error that is misclassified.
- The unload polling budget does not encompass every initial/final `launchctl print` or bootout call (`src/tunnel-service.ts:143-152`; service equivalent `src/service.ts:51-57`). This is the already documented timing limitation, not a new finding.
- Account-side connector creation, native approval, live tunnel ownership, and installed-app behavior cannot be inferred from these service helpers. No service source path changes the public connector ABI.
