## Final disposition

One concrete remaining source defect was found and corrected. The correction is limited to the supervisor's tunnel-control queue handoff.

### T3-15-1 — a queued recovery command could outwait its cancellation signal

- **Trigger:** A tunnel control child is unresolved after the wave2 bounded termination path, and a later command calls `runTunnelCommand(..., recoverySignal)` while waiting on `previousControl`.
- **Defect:** The wave2 queue serialized the later command with `await previousControl`, but that wait had no cancellation race. If the predecessor never emitted `close`, the later startup or recovery caller could remain suspended indefinitely and never reject on its aborted recovery signal. That could exceed the caller's bounded startup/shutdown settlement contract.
- **Correction:** The queued wait now races the predecessor gate against the supplied recovery signal. On cancellation it rejects immediately, while attaching the queued slot's release to the predecessor gate. This keeps later commands blocked until the predecessor's `close`-backed ownership gate settles, so cancellation does not trade the hang for control-process overlap. The existing child registry, bounded termination, pipe-error handling, and wave1 stale-owner signal propagation remain intact.
