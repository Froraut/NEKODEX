## Applied correction

### Disabled-account inspection was incorrectly rejected

The new evidence epoch guard in `evidenceIsCurrent` already prevents an asynchronous inspection from republishing evidence after `setAccountEnabled` invalidates the account's epoch. Its additional requirement that the account currently be enabled caused a concrete UI/API inconsistency:

1. `AccountSettings.tsx` exposes Check and Check connector for disabled accounts.
2. `checkAccount` can inspect such an account after the existing active-operation checks.
3. A successful inspection then failed the pool's enabled-state check with “ChatGPT account readiness changed while checking it.”
4. The account could therefore not be inspected until it was enabled, even though the UI permits the operation.

The fix keeps the epoch equality check and removes only the redundant current-enabled check. Disabling an account still immediately clears capability/connector evidence and advances its epoch. A check that was already in flight cannot repopulate evidence after disable/re-enable because the epoch has changed. A disabled account remains excluded by `eligible()` from new automatic turns, and enabling it invalidates any evidence again through `setAccountEnabled`.
